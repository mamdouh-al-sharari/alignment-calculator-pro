Reference Direction — Top View
Operator position: Stand BEHIND THE MOVABLE MOTOR and look toward the FIXED PUMP. In this drawing, the pump is on the LEFT, the motor on the RIGHT, and your viewing direction is RIGHT → LEFT. Facing the pump: 9 o'clock = your LEFT; 3 o'clock = your RIGHT. Use this reference for the Rim & Face instructions below.
Reverse Dial Calculator
D1 = distance between Dial A and Dial B. D2 = distance from Dial A to Front Feet. D3 = distance from Dial A to Back Feet. D2 and D3 are measured from Dial A.
Reverse Dial readings: Zero A at 12 o’clock and B at 6 o’clock, then rotate 180° and record A at 6 and B at 12. For horizontal readings, zero A at 3 and B at 9, then rotate 180° and record A at 9 and B at 3. Zero reference readings are implicit; enter signed readings only. B top is converted to the common bottom-reference sign for calculation.
Rim & Face Calculator - Formula Method
Measurement setup: Stand behind the movable MOTOR facing the stationary PUMP. Both indicators are fixed to the motor shaft, tips touch the pump hub. Vertical: zero at 12 o'clock and rotate to 6. Horizontal: zero at 3 and rotate to 9. Read and enter signed readings. 9 o'clock is LEFT from behind motor. D: full face dial travel diameter. X: rim contact to FRONT motor foot. Y: rim contact directly to BACK motor foot.
Dimension units: D, X and Y are measured in cm. Dial readings and correction results are in mm.
